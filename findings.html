TikTok Data Visualizations

To better understand how the mock neck trend performs on TikTok, we analyzed two scraped datasets: one focused on general mock neck content and one focused specifically on PARKE-related posts. We compared views, likes, comments and caption themes to identify patterns in audience behavior. These visuals show that visibility alone does not fully explain trend growth. Instead, repeated engagement, recognizable aesthetics, influencer-driven branding and audience participation all help determine which posts perform well and continue spreading across the platform.

Average Engagement by Dataset

This chart compares average views, likes and comments between the general mock neck dataset and the PARKE-specific dataset. The comparison helps show the difference between broad trend visibility and brand-focused interaction. General mock neck content may reach a wider audience because it is not limited to one brand, but PARKE-related posts can still create strong engagement because viewers recognize the product and connect it to a specific trend identity.

Most Common Caption Themes

This chart shows the most common caption themes that appeared in the mock neck TikTok posts we collected. Caption language matters because it helps frame how viewers understand the trend. Words connected to outfits, styling, brands, comfort and wanting the product show that the mock neck is being discussed as both a fashion item and a social media trend. These repeated themes help explain how TikTok captions contribute to trend visibility and make posts easier for audiences to recognize.

Engagement Rate Comparison

This chart compares engagement rates by looking at likes and comments in relation to views. This is important because a video with a large number of views is not always the most meaningful example of audience interest. Engagement rate gives a better sense of how actively viewers responded to the content. A higher engagement rate suggests that users were more likely to like, comment or interact with the post instead of simply scrolling past it.

Top 10 TikToks by Views

This chart highlights the highest-viewed TikToks across both datasets. These posts help show which types of mock neck videos gained the most visibility. Looking at the top posts also helps separate reach from interaction. Some videos may have performed well because they used humor, relatable situations or popular outfit formats, while others may have gained attention because of brand recognition. This shows that the mock neck trend spreads through a mix of aesthetics, entertainment value and audience familiarity.

Key Takeaway

Across both datasets, our findings show that higher engagement is not only about views. A post can reach a large audience, but the trend becomes stronger when viewers actively respond through likes, comments, shares and product interest. The mock neck trend gained visibility because it was repeated across outfit videos, brand-specific posts and relatable TikTok formats. PARKE content especially shows how a recognizable brand can turn a general fashion trend into something more specific and desirable. Overall, these findings suggest that TikTok trend growth depends on both visibility and interaction.
